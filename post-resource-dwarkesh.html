Dwarkesh Patel × Dario Amodei · Feb 13 2026 · 2h 22m

Why it's worth 2h 22m of your life

The clearest framing of the two exponentials (technical capability and economic adoption) and the Anthropic investment paradox: spend billions on training now or fall behind, but overspending kills you before revenue arrives. Revenue ramp $0 → $100M → $1B → $9-10B in three years.

We are near the end of the exponential. Not because the technology stops improving, but because the economic and physical constraints catch up.

The MBA-relevant bits

Whole conversation maps onto INSEAD frameworks if you're paying attention:

  • Prices & Markets oligopoly — three labs, all spending the same, all racing. Classic textbook setup.
  • POM newsvendor — Anthropic's training-spend decision is a newsvendor problem at $10B scale. Spend too little, fall behind. Spend too much, run out of cash.
  • Demand elasticity — when does the market split into "API for builders" and "Claude.ai for end users" with different price-points?

The line that stuck

"50% of entry-level white-collar jobs eliminated within 1-5 years."

Not stated as a hope. Stated as a forecast. He could be wrong. He's also the person with the best information.

  • 1:10-1:40 — the investment paradox and revenue ramp
  • 2:00-end — labour market predictions
