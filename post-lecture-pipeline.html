Lecture recording → polished study notes, end to end

II Isabella Isotta · 05 April 2026 · 4 min read
#lecture-notes #whisper #obsidian #gemini

End-to-end automated. Phone records the lecture, polished study notes appear in my vault by the time I'm home. Five steps, each doing one thing.

The pipeline

  1. iPhone Shortcut records as [Course]_L##.m4a to iCloud.
  2. Bash wrapper moves the audio to Google Drive.
  3. Python script uploads it to Gemini 2.5 Flash (verbatim transcript, professor labelled "Professor:", auto-continues if the token limit is hit).
  4. Bash routing drops the .txt into the right Sources/L##/ folder.
  5. Claude via my /update skill detects the unprocessed transcript, hands it to lecture-note-processor, which generates Key Concepts / Summary / Detailed Notes and updates the wikilink graph.

Why split it

One giant prompt loses the plot. Splitting into stages was way more reliable. If one stage breaks, you re-run that stage, not the whole thing.

The trick that made it sound like me

Feeding Claude my existing notes as context, not just the new transcript. The output sounds like me because it has 200 of my prior notes to imitate.

Privacy

Only Apple, Google, and Anthropic touch the data. No third parties.

Time saved

About three hours per lecture. ~200 hours saved across the MBA. By a wide margin the highest-leverage thing I've built.
