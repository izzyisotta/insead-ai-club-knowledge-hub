The Shape of AI: jaggedness, bottlenecks and salients

Recommended by II Isabella Isotta · 15 April 2026
#ethan-mollick #frontier #worth-reading
↗ One Useful Thing · oneusefulthing.org

By Ethan Mollick · One Useful Thing · 12 min read

Why this is worth your time

Mollick's clearest framing yet of the uneven AI frontier. AI is extraordinary at some tasks and bafflingly bad at adjacent ones. The practical question is which "salients" you can ride, and which "bottlenecks" still require humans.

Most useful for INSEAD: the salient/bottleneck distinction maps directly onto our courses. Strategy case prep — salient. Quant courses with handwritten work — bottleneck. Knowing which is which saves you a term of trial-and-error.

The frontier is jagged. Some tasks are far easier for AI than you'd expect. Others are surprisingly hard. The skill isn't using AI. It's knowing where the jaggedness is.

How the AI Club is using it

This vocabulary is about to anchor the next round of Show Your Workflow sessions. Each presenter starts with: here's the salient I'm riding, here's the bottleneck I still hit. Cleaner than "here's the cool tool I built."
