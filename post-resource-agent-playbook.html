Why this is the right rec for the cohort

Most actionable on-ramp I've seen. Anti-hype, very specific. If someone in the cohort says "I want to build an agent but don't know where to start," send them this.

A chatbot waits for your next message. An agent gets a goal, makes a plan, uses tools, checks what happened, adjusts, and keeps going until the work is done or it needs your help.

The playbook

  1. Pick one narrow job. "Book me a court when I message 'tennis with X'" not "be my life assistant."
  2. Small loop. 4-5 steps, not 50.
  3. Few clean tools. Calendar, browser, one CRM. Not a toolkit of 30.
  4. Hard stop conditions. Confirmed slot booked, OR 2 minutes elapsed, OR 5 retries failed → stop.

What I love about it

The compose-don't-stack philosophy. Same thing Freddie says about his Mac Mini setup: each capability is ordinary, the combination is what makes it feel different. This article says it crisply enough to send to non-technical classmates.
