Why this is the share

i urge everyone to read this.

An 8-page memo from Block. The thesis: hierarchies were a way to allocate scarce intelligence across decisions. When intelligence becomes abundant, hierarchies become a bottleneck, not a feature.

The roles section

Roles aren't job descriptions. They're constraints on what information you handle and what decisions you make. When Claude can do most of the synthesis a manager used to do, the role of "manager" fragments. Some functions move down (decision-makers don't need a layer between them and the data). Some move sideways (specialists who used to report up now collaborate horizontally). Some disappear.

This is what Block has been quietly piloting internally. Not a structural reorg — a slow re-allocation of who actually decides what.

Why the cohort should read it

You're about to walk into organisations whose org charts were designed for the Drucker era. The companies that survive the next five years aren't the ones with better AI. They're the ones who recognise that "manage" isn't the verb it used to be.

Read it: @fromhierarchytointelligence.
