Why this is the share

Blackstone and Goldman backing a $1.5bn JV with Anthropic is the moment enterprise finance fully commits. Not a tech-sector bet — a financial-system bet. The biggest balance sheets in the world are now structurally exposed to Claude.

It really is an exciting time to be alive.

What this means for the cohort

Consulting Claude services to enterprise will be the most in-demand skill in the next 2 years. Not building models. Not even building agents. Helping large organisations actually deploy them.

The strategy curve looks like this: Big Four and the bulge bracket build practices. Mid-market consultancies pivot. Boutiques specialise by industry. By 2027 every PE shop has an internal AI ops lead.

If you're MBB or coming out of one, this is the wave. If you're INSEAD-bound for tech, this is the bridge between AI and finance you've been waiting for.

The structural read

Anthropic's revenue ramp ($0 → $100M → $1B → $9-10B in three years) is now Blackstone-sponsored. The capex side (compute, data centres) gets the Goldman/BX backing it needs to keep up with the model side. The two exponentials Dario described on Dwarkesh just got a financing structure.
